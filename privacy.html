Privacy Policy

Who we are

Trendy Tools Hub is operated by Shivam Negi, an individual based in India. Contact: Shivamnegi9557@gmail.com

What our application does

Our application publishes Pins to a single Pinterest business account — our own — using the official Pinterest API. It reads the boards belonging to that same account in order to publish to the correct board.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, request, store, sell, or process personal data belonging to any other Pinterest user. We have no users other than the account owner. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or visitor tracking.

Credentials

The access token for our own Pinterest account is stored encrypted in GitHub Actions Secrets and is used solely to publish our own Pins. It is not shared with any third party.

Third-party services

Pin text is generated using the Google Gemini API and Pin images using Pollinations.ai. Publishing notifications are sent to the account owner only, via Telegram. None of these services receive personal data about Pinterest users.

Affiliate disclosure

Pins may contain affiliate links, including Amazon Associates links. We earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. Every such Pin carries a disclosure in its description.
